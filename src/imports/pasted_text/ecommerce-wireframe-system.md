Create a complete ecommerce wireframe system for a premium European / Danish Scandinavian furniture and home decor website inspired by ferm LIVING’s UX structure, but do not copy their branding, logo, images, exact copy, or visual assets.

Design style:
Minimal, warm Scandinavian, premium editorial ecommerce. Use lots of white space, soft off-white background, charcoal text, muted beige/sand/clay accents, elegant typography, clean product grids, large lifestyle imagery placeholders, refined spacing, subtle dividers, and simple text-based CTAs.

Create wireframes for desktop and mobile.

Global navigation:
Build a sticky header with:
- Left: brand logo placeholder
- Main nav: Shop, Inspiration, Rooms, Professionals, New Collection
- Right: Search, Wishlist, Cart with count, Login, Language/Currency selector
- Mega menu with multi-column categories

- New collection product carousel
- Featured room inspiration section
- Kitchen or furniture editorial banner
- Bestsellers product grid
- Newsletter signup
- Trust benefit bar
- Footer

2. Collection / Product Listing Page
Sections:
- Page title and short intro
- Filter sidebar: category, room, price, color, material, size, availability, new, certified
- Sort dropdown
- Product grid, 4 columns desktop, 2 tablet, 1–2 mobile
- Product cards with image, hover image state, badge, product name, price, color dots, quick add, wishlist

- Related products carousel
- User-generated content hashtag section
